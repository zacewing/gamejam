Tune tips
Street build: Keep boost/load around 35–60% and timing/fuel near the middle. This is the safest place to start.
Power build: Raise boost first, then fuel. Add timing last. More power is possible, but reliability falls quickly when all three are pushed.
Efficiency: The “Street-safe tune” caps aggressive settings. It gives up some peak power but makes your build much harder to hurt on the dyno.
Part strategy: Spending more is not automatically better. Cooling and reliability parts can let you safely run a stronger tune than a cheap all-power setup.
